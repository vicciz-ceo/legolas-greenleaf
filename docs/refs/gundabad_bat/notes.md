# Gundabad bat modelling notes

Original giant bat with rat-like head, two naturally strut-supported leathery wings, two hind feet and coarse grey-brown fur. Original wingspan 6–8 m selects 7 m. Side is generated first; frontal wing tips are the visible calibration endpoints. Side height 3.5 m, oblique width 6 m and folded height 2 m are explicitly authored projected targets, not recovered 3D measurements.

Spread-wing flight and upside-down hanging folded-wing rest are creature pose exceptions. The folded pose uses its own 768×512 panel at the main flight-sheet scale, with no generated perch or comparison figure. All human silhouettes, geometry, backgrounds, rulers and labels come from code.

Three-quarter body attempt 1 repeated the lateral angle; attempt 2 passed using the accepted side and front as the minimum angle references. Other body views and head portraits passed first attempts. Natural membrane and claw replace clothing/metal detail slots; skin and fur tiles reuse individually reviewed source crops. No outfit or armour applies. Minor vein/strand and lighting drift is tolerated. Samples are rendered and lit, not albedo. No mirrored source or generated text. Full-resolution originals are archived outside this committed library with sources.json provenance.

# Haradrim modelling notes

Original adult painted warm-brown face, red turban/cowl and red-black robes, short black beard, gold jewellery and own-right scimitar. Original brief does not specify footwear; dark boots are authored intent.

Front attempt 1 had narrow arm separation; accepted front 2 has a relaxed A-pose and five visible digits. Side attempt 1 had an oblique torso; accepted side 2 is profile. Other body and all portrait views passed on attempt 1. No mirrored sources or generated text. The four-view 64-pixel silhouette strip is readable.

Minor embroidery, robe folds, jewellery visibility and light drift are tolerated. Scalp hair is concealed; the hair tile and observations use the visible beard. Covered scalp is recorded as not visible, with no invented pixel value. Every garment and metal has a reviewed sample rectangle. Samples are rendered and lit, not albedo; design palette remains authored intent. Do not infer 3D anatomy from normalization.

Code supplies canvas, labels, quarter-metre rulers, common scale and baseline. Full-resolution originals and crop provenance are in sources.json.

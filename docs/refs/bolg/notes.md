# Bolg modelling notes

- Preserve the defining silhouette: huge pale orc, bolted skull and jaw, rusted spikes, right-hand mace.
- Treat 2.60 m as the body-height design target and 2.60 m as the overall normalization target including headgear.
- Use these attachment sides from the character's own body: {'right_hand': 'mace', 'left_hand': 'empty', 'skull_and_jaw': 'bolted iron plates and braces'}.
- Keep the face original: heavy brow, small eyes, flattened nose, uneven fangs and dark old scars. Avoid any actor likeness.
- Hair silhouette: bald scarred scalp with flush bolted plates and jaw braces; build fine strands rather than a solid helmet of hair.
- Materials: ragged dark kilt, coarse leather straps, worn foot wraps; heavy rusted iron spiked pauldrons, bracers and shin plates; skull and jaw plates bolted into skin.
- Avoid: ordinary removable helmet, smooth unscarred skin, dwarf proportions, actor likeness.
- Design dimensions and PBR values are intent. The compositor records image normalization; observed colours are rendered and lit, not albedo.
- Tolerate minor buckle, seam and strap-count drift and mild lighting differences. Keep attachment sides, anatomy, gear inventory and view angle consistent.

- Three portraits reuse author-reviewed native head-and-shoulders crops from their corresponding accepted body originals, without mirroring or new generation. The scalp is bald: its detail tile shows bolted scalp plates, and observed hair colour is explicitly null.

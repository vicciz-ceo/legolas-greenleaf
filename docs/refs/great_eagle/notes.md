# Great eagle modelling notes

Original golden-brown giant raptor, 10 m wingspan, natural feathered wings/tail, curved yellow beak and two tucked taloned feet. Original roster explicitly requires one flight silhouette sheet only: no portraits, details or sampling. Side generated first; accepted frontal wing-tip extrema supply wingspan calibration. Top span 10 m, oblique width 8.5 m and side flight height 5 m are authored projected assumptions, not recovered 3D anatomy.

Side, front and top passed first attempts. Three-quarter attempts 1 and 2 repeated the lateral angle; attempt 3 gives a frontal-oblique chest and head using the accepted front plus side for identity. No mirrored views or generated text. Feather count and mild lighting drift are tolerated. Full-resolution originals remain archived with sources.json provenance. All comparisons, geometry, ruler and labels are code-drawn.

# Mirkwood spider modelling notes

Original black-brown hairy spider with patterned bulbous abdomen, eight jointed legs, eight glossy eyes and paired mandibles. Body height about 1.2 m and selected leg span 2.75 m are design targets from the brief (ARCHITECTURE gives approximate 2.5 m span). The sheet measures outer leg-tip alpha endpoints, not 3D anatomy. Side span is fore-aft; front/top left-right, and the oblique projected extent is authored intent.

Body front, side and top passed first attempts. Three-quarter attempt 1 repeated the side angle; accepted attempt 2 has a distinct front-oblique angle. Front head attempt 1 had six eyes and was rejected; accepted attempt 2 has eight eyes arranged 2+4+2. Other portrait and independent abdomen/mandible detail calls passed first attempts. Surface and bristle tiles are separately reviewed close-up crops of the accepted abdomen detail.

Creature exception: no garments, manufactured armour or weapons. Detail labels describe chitin, mandibles and coarse bristles. The schema skin observation is a chitin surface proxy; the hair rectangle samples an actual coarse bristle. All observations are rendered and lit, not albedo. Minor bristle/pattern and lighting drift is tolerated. No generated labels or mirrored views. Full sheet and 64-pixel silhouette previews pass review.

All retained body and portrait cutouts were uniformly resized for the 2.5 MB budget; sources.json records before/after sizes and original full-resolution paths. Final labels, grey background, 1.85 m human comparisons and ruler are drawn in code.

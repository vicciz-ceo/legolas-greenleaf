# Mumak modelling notes

Original war oliphaunt: four natural legs, one trunk, four huge ivory tusks in two pairs, wrinkled grey-brown hide, red paint, wooden howdah, red/gold banners, lashings and broad girths. Rope ladder stays on own LEFT flank. Frontal body and head views explicitly show four tusks; profile views naturally overlap/occlude far tusks. Sparse body bristles and dark tail tuft are authored natural detail.

Original scale is 14 m at the shoulder. The reviewed retained-side shoulder region immediately behind the ear and below the deck excludes the tower/head/ear tops; compose.py computes its alpha endpoints. Front/oblique total heights and overhead fore-aft length are derived in code from reference overall bounds normalized to this shoulder span. These are documented 2D projection assumptions, not recovered 3D anatomy. The howdah intentionally extends above the head, an original-roster creature exception to humanoid held-gear head limits.

Side/front body views passed first attempts. Top attempt 1 was high-frontal; attempt 2 clipped the tail tuft; accepted attempt 3 is overhead with complete curved tail. Three-quarter attempt 1 repeated the side angle; accepted attempt 2 is front-oblique. All three head portraits passed first attempts. Howdah detail attempt 1 clipped the ladder end; accepted attempt 2 shows the complete ladder. Ivory, hide and tail-hair tiles are reviewed crops of accepted individual sources. A small post-intake crop removes stray flank from the tail-hair tile; it is documented in sources.json.

Final sheets and 64-pixel silhouettes pass review. Minor paint strokes, banner motifs, rope counts and lighting drift are tolerated. No mirrored sources or generated labels. Every garment/metal has a reviewed retained-source rectangle; gold banner trim is textile, ivory is biological, and observed pixels are rendered and lit, not albedo. Full-resolution originals remain on disk with provenance. The accepted howdah close-up is reused for the separately validated supplemental PNG/JPEG.

# Prop sheets

Six sheets cover 26 props. Each front/three-quarter view pairs with a 1.85 m silhouette; panel zoom varies across sizes and is never a shared pixel/metre scale. The JSON is the geometry authority. Banners are original generic heraldic interpretations; no official film vector art. Dimensions are design targets, not canonical measurements.

Ladders: 0.85 m rail width, 0.30 m rung spacing, 0.05 m rung diameter, 22 m total length for 20 m wall. Siege tower: 28 m with 2.5 m wheel diameter, 6 m deployment bridge. Ram: 8 m timber length and 0.55 m log diameter, six carry pairs. Boat: 5 m length, 1.7 m beam, 0.35 m loaded draft. Nets: 50–80 mm knots/mesh; silk sheets: 70–90% gaps. Lantern glass is procedural transparent material. No lettering on Balin’s tomb in these references.

All scale figures, labels and true-size bounding boxes are drawn by code. Transparent looks are cropped using measured alpha bounds (or a measured background threshold), then fitted to orthographic design dimensions. Three-quarter widths use the explicit 45° bounding target; appearance poses are illustrative. Rung spacing, banner cloth thickness and web openness remain numeric construction targets.

The siege ladder uses code-rendered binding rung geometry (72 rungs at 0.30 m spacing) with the generated timber swatch supplying surface appearance. This avoids treating the image model’s irregular rung count as a construction measurement.

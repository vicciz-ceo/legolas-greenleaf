# Legolas modelling notes

- Keep the tall slender silhouette, long pale-gold hair and low left-hand recurve bow readable at 64 px.
- Preserve full high cheekbones and the original softly squared chin; avoid hollow cheeks or any actor likeness.
- Almond blue eyes need upper and lower lids, natural brows and a straight refined nose bridge.
- Model the two narrow temple braids behind pointed ears and the long hair falling down the back; do not make pure white hair.
- Mount quiver on the character's RIGHT back, white fletchings above the right shoulder but below the crown; put BOTH white-handled knives beside it, sheathed with grips up.
- The bow stays in the character's LEFT hand; front image-right becomes back image-left. Never mirror a view.
- Tunic ends at mid-thigh; the fitted stitched leather jerkin ends above it. Keep bracer and soft boot wear fine rather than exaggerated.
- Tolerated drift: buckle shapes, leaf stitching and a slight change in braid width or loose hair waves. Preserve gear inventory and attachment sides.
- Turnaround scale is normalized to overall 1.85 m. Face portraits have no metric scale claim. Design proportions and PBR parameters remain targets; observed colours are rendered and lit, not albedo.
- Lost-source recovery: current retained images are newly authored from the preserved original specification. Historical attempts remain in progress.json; replacements use a separate capped series.

- face_views/side: accepted single-view body original reused with an author-reviewed head-and-shoulders crop; no new angle or identity generated. Portrait call history is preserved.
